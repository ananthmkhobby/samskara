# Using Samskara on WhatsApp

This is the message to send a family member who's new to the WhatsApp side of
Samskara — paste it into the family group, or forward it to them directly.

## Getting started

Just send the Samskara WhatsApp number anything — a photo, a voice note, or a
text message. The first time you write in, you'll get a one-time secure link
to connect your WhatsApp number to your Samskara account. Open it, confirm,
and you're connected — no password needed on WhatsApp itself.

**One extra step the first time:** your account also needs to know *which
person in the family tree you are*. Until that's set, Samskara can save what
you send, but a couple of shortcuts (see below) won't know who to apply to.
Ask your Family Head or an Admin to open **Admin → Members** in the app and
set it for you — it's a one-time, 10-second fix. (If you manage the account
yourself: look for **"Which one is you in the tree?"** under your own name in
that same Members list.)

> **If you have more than one Samskara login** (for example, a separate
> account you use for app admin vs. your personal one), make sure it's the
> login tied to *your WhatsApp number* that gets linked — not just whichever
> one happens to already show your name. Admin → Members lists every login
> for the family; if you're not sure which one WhatsApp is using, just send
> any message and ask a Head/Admin to check which row lit up.

## What you can send

| You send | What happens |
|---|---|
| A photo | Samskara asks who it's of, then whether you remember anything about it, then asks **private or shared**, then confirms before saving. |
| A voice note | Same flow — you can even answer "who is this about" or tell the story by voice instead of typing. |
| A document (PDF) | Same flow, saved as a document instead of a photo. |
| Just text | Saved as a memory, same flow. |
| `SKIP` (when asked who it's about) | Saves it as an unassigned family memory — still saved, just not linked to a person yet. |
| `CANCEL` (any time) | Throws away whatever's in progress. Nothing is saved. |
| `FAMILY` | If you belong to more than one family tree, switches which one you're currently saving to. |

Every save — photo, voice note, document, or memory — ends with **"Private
to just you, or shared with the family? Reply PRIVATE or SHARE."** Private
means only you will ever see it (under Gallery → Private to me in the app).
Nothing is saved until you then reply **YES** to the final confirmation.

If you're not a Head or Admin, what you send goes to the family's review
queue first rather than appearing immediately — same as adding something
from the app.

## The "profile pic" shortcut

Want to update your own photo on the tree? Send the photo with the caption:

```
profile pic
```

It updates your tree/Folio photo **immediately** — no review queue, and you
can do this as often as you like. Your previous photo isn't deleted; it's
kept privately, visible only to you, under Gallery → Private to me.

This needs your account to already be linked to a person in the tree (see
"Getting started" above) — if it isn't, Samskara will tell you and ask you to
get it linked first.

## For Heads and Admins: updating someone else's photo

If you're a Family Head or Admin, you can update **anyone's** tree photo on
their behalf — handy for an older relative who isn't on WhatsApp themselves.
Send the photo captioned:

```
profile pic for <their name>
```

for example `profile pic for Lakshmi` or `profile picture of M K Ananth`.
Samskara looks their name up in the tree:
- **One match** → updates immediately, and keeps their previous photo in the
  family Gallery (not deleted).
- **No match** → tells you it couldn't find them; try their full name.
- **More than one match** → lists the names it found; resend with the full
  name to disambiguate.

Only a Head or Admin can do this for someone else — anyone else trying it
gets a polite "only a Head or Admin can do that" reply instead.

## Troubleshooting

- **"We don't know which person in the tree you are yet"** — see "Getting
  started" above; ask a Head/Admin to link your account to your name under
  Admin → Members.
- **Nothing happens / no reply** — Samskara always replies, even on failure
  ("I couldn't save this right now, nothing has been lost"). If you truly get
  no reply at all, wait a minute and resend — it's safe to resend, duplicates
  are automatically ignored.
- **It asked about the wrong photo** — reply `CANCEL` and send it again fresh.
